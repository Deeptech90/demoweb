Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first visit. Don't see your question? Contact us anytime.

We recommend a professional cleaning and examination every 6 months for most patients. Those with gum disease, a history of cavities, or other risk factors may benefit from visits every 3–4 months. Our team will create a personalized care schedule based on your unique oral health needs.

Invisalign is generally much more comfortable than traditional braces. You may feel mild pressure when switching to a new set of aligners — this is normal and typically fades within 1–2 days. Treatment duration varies from 6 to 18 months depending on case complexity. Most patients see noticeable results within the first few months.

Yes! We accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, United Healthcare, MetLife, and more. We also offer in-house financing plans with 0% interest for qualified patients, and flexible monthly payment options through CareCredit. Our team will verify your benefits before treatment.

With proper care, dental implants can last a lifetime. The titanium implant post fuses with your jawbone in a process called osseointegration, making it incredibly stable and durable. The crown (visible tooth) typically lasts 10–15 years before needing replacement due to normal wear. Brushing, flossing, and regular checkups are key to longevity.

Professional teeth whitening performed in our office is completely safe when done correctly. Unlike over-the-counter products, we use clinically tested formulations at precise concentrations with custom-fitted trays to protect your gums and enamel. Temporary tooth sensitivity is common for 24–48 hours post-treatment and resolves on its own. We don't recommend whitening for those with severe sensitivity or active decay.

Call us immediately at (800) 555-0199. We offer same-day emergency appointments during business hours and an after-hours emergency line for urgent situations. If you've knocked out a tooth, keep it moist (place it in milk or between your cheek and gum) and come in immediately — the faster you act, the better the chance of saving it.
